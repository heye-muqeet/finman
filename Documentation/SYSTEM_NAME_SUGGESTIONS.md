2. **.io** - Popular for tech startups, "input/output" connotation
3. **.finance** - Industry-specific, professional
4. **.com** - Traditional, most recognized (often taken)
5. **.ai** - Great if AI is a key feature
6. **.tech** - Technology-focused
7. **.dev** - Development/tech community

## Domain Registration Tips

- **Register for multiple years** (often cheaper)
- **Enable privacy protection** (WHOIS privacy)
- **Set up auto-renewal** (avoid losing domain)
- **Consider multiple TLDs** (.app, .io, .com) to protect brand
- **Check renewal prices** (some registrars have high renewal fees)
