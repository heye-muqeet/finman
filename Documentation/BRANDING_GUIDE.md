# FinMan - Branding Guide

## Brand Identity

**System Name**: FinMan  
**Full Name**: Financial Manager  
**Domain**: finman.finance  
**Tagline**: "Your Personal Financial Manager"  
**Slogan Options**:
- "Your Personal Financial Manager"
- "Manage Your Money, Master Your Future"
- "Smart Finance, Simple Management"
- "Take Control of Your Finances"

## Logo Concepts

### Design Ideas
1. **Modern Minimalist**: "FM" monogram with financial chart line
2. **Icon-Based**: Shield or briefcase icon with "FM" integration
3. **Abstract**: Flowing lines representing financial flow
4. **Professional**: Clean typography with subtle financial symbol

### Color Palette Suggestions

#### Primary Colors
- **Primary Blue**: #2563EB (Professional, trustworthy)
- **Primary Green**: #10B981 (Financial growth, success)
- **Dark Blue**: #1E40AF (Depth, stability)

#### Secondary Colors
- **Accent Orange**: #F59E0B (Energy, action)
- **Neutral Gray**: #6B7280 (Balance, professionalism)
- **Light Gray**: #F3F4F6 (Background, space)

#### Recommended Combination
- **Primary**: #2563EB (Blue)
- **Secondary**: #10B981 (Green)
- **Accent**: #F59E0B (Orange)
- **Text**: #1F2937 (Dark Gray)
- **Background**: #FFFFFF (White) / #F9FAFB (Light Gray)

## Typography

### Font Recommendations
- **Primary Font**: Inter, Roboto, or Poppins (Modern, readable)
- **Headings**: Montserrat or Raleway (Bold, professional)
- **Monospace**: Fira Code or JetBrains Mono (For numbers/data)

### Font Sizes
- **Logo**: 24-32px
- **Headings**: 24px, 20px, 18px
- **Body**: 16px
- **Small Text**: 14px, 12px

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Characteristics
- **Professional** yet approachable
- **Clear** and straightforward
- **Trustworthy** and reliable
- **Modern** and innovative
- **Helpful** and supportive

### Communication Style
- Use clear, simple language
- Avoid financial jargon when possible
- Be encouraging and positive
- Focus on user empowerment
- Emphasize ease of use

## Brand Guidelines Summary

### Do's ✅
- Use consistent colors and fonts
- Maintain professional appearance

- Don't use overly casual language

- Don't use generic financial imagery
- Don't forget mobile optimization
